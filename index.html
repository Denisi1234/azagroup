Who We Are

We are one of the most diversified and progressive family businesses in the Middle East, proudly headquartered in Dubai, United Arab Emirates.

With a portfolio spanning Automotive, Retail, Real Estate, Health, Finance, and Education, our 40,000+ people are united by a shared purpose: Enriching Lives Every Day.

We are committed to delivering the highest quality products and services for today and tomorrow — as an AI-empowered company and workplace, driving innovation, creating unique experiences, and building future readiness across all our sectors.
