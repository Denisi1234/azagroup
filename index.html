Who We Are

Aza Group is a Tanzanian technology company based in Dar es Salaam. Our mission is to close the digital divide and make technology easier for people and businesses to use.

We build practical digital products for everyday needs, from managing business accounts to booking a place to stay. We also help organisations build software, make better use of their data, and set up secure networks.
